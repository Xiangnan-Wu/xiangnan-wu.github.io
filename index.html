1.1

Xiangnan Wu

吴祥楠 · Multimodal Understanding & Embodied Intelligence

Ph.D. Candidate in Artificial Intelligence UCAS & NLPR, CASIA · Beijing

Building generalist intelligent agents that perceive, reason, and act across both physical and virtual worlds.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at UCAS & NLPR@CASIA, advised by Yan Huang and Liang Wang.

My research centers on Embodied Intelligence (Robotics & Game Agents) and Multimodal Models. My long-term goal is to develop generalist intelligent agents that assist people across both physical and virtual worlds, currently focusing on VLM as Policy and World Models.

2.1

Research Focus

I. Multimodal Understanding 2.1

Encoding linguistic semantics and visual signals within a shared latent space, enabling neural networks to comprehend the physical world across both spatial and temporal dimensions.

II. Embodied Intelligence 2.2

Leveraging upstream multimodal understanding to empower embodied agents—translating rich perceptual representations into world modeling, reasoning, and closed-loop action policies across physical robots and virtual environments.
